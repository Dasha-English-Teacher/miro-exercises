December 2 · Christmas Advent

The next day

1

Read what happened to Clara the next day. Is it similar to your guess?

Clara woke up to the sound of voices outside her window. Still groggy, she pulled back the curtain and froze—police cars were parked right next door, lights flashing. Her stomach tightened as she watched officers moving in and out of her neighbor’s house, setting up a yellow tape around the yard. Unable to shake her curiosity, Clara slipped on her shoes and went outside.

A few neighbors were whispering together, and she approached a woman she recognized from down the street. “Do you know what happened?” Clara asked. The woman leaned in, her voice low. “They say Mr. Green has gone missing. No one’s seen him for days.” Clara’s heart skipped a beat. Mr. Green? Missing? Just yesterday, she’d written about Lily’s neighbor disappearing. Her mind raced, a mix of shock and creeping suspicion settling in. Could this be a coincidence? Or was it somehow connected to her story?
