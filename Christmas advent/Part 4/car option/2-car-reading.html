The Car in the Driveway

02 · Read

Not quite what she pictured

Is Clara generally positive or negative about how her wish turned out?

Read and decide.

Clara woke up and stared out her window in surprise. There, in her driveway, was a car—a worn old hatchback with faded paint and a rusty hood.

It wasn’t exactly what she’d pictured, but she’d never had a car before, and she definitely couldn’t afford one. Maybe this would be okay?

Curious, she slipped outside. The inside smelled a little funny, and the seats were worn. She turned the key. Yes, there it was. A car of her own.

Nervous but excited, she decided to take it for a quick drive. At first, every bump made her a bit uneasy, she even got a feeling something was broken, but as she drove to the nearby grocery store, Clara felt a growing sense of freedom.
