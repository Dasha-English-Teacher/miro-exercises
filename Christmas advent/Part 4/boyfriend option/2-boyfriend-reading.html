The man behind the wish

Is Clara generally positive or negative about how her wish turned out? Read and decide.

Clara spent the whole day looking out the window, wondering what her new boyfriend looked like. She’d listened to his voice message a hundred times, trying to imagine his face, but nothing quite matched. She tried to stay calm, reminding herself this whole situation was supposed to be exciting—but something felt off.

Finally, as the sky turned dusky, she saw him walking down the path towards the house. He was bundled up in a worn winter jacket, with his tired eyes and friendly face visible just beneath his slightly receding hairline. Her heart sank a little. He looked kind, sure, but… this wasn’t what she had pictured.

When he stepped through the door, he smiled warmly and gave her a gentle hug, as if everything was completely normal between them. Clara managed to smile back, though her mind was racing. She wanted to know more about him without making it seem like she’d forgotten their entire “relationship.”

“So, uh… how was work today?” she asked, keeping her tone casual.

“Oh, same as always,” he replied, pulling off his gloves. “You know how it is at the warehouse, nothing too exciting.”

Clara nodded. A warehouse? She had hoped for something a little more impressive. But as he spoke, she couldn’t help noticing how gentle he seemed, how he paid full attention to her every word. He offered to make her tea, asked if she’d had a good day, and made a point to tell her about a little pastry shop he’d found that he thought she’d like. Despite her disappointment, she couldn’t deny that he was kind. And maybe, just maybe, that counted for something.
