Reveal the story

Clara woke on a hard, narrow bunk in a dark concrete room. The smell of mold hung in the air, and a weak glow came through a barred window high on the wall. Clara was overwhelmed with frustration. Then she noticed an older woman sitting across from her.

“¿Dónde estoy?” Clara asked, startled by how easily the Spanish came to her.

The woman’s eyes widened. “¿No sabes dónde estás?”

Looking desperate, Clara started crying.

“No. ¿Dónde exactamente?”

The woman looked at Clara with concern. “Estás en México, en prisión. Yo he estado aquí tres años,” she said, her tone shifting from amused to serious. Clara froze, tears welling up. She wanted to escape her old life, but now she was in a foreign prison without even remembering how she got there.

Useful vocabulary

overwhelmed with frustration — extremely frustrated
startled — suddenly surprised
her tone shifted — the way she spoke changed
tears welling up — tears beginning to fill her eyes
at the expense of — involving the loss of something else
