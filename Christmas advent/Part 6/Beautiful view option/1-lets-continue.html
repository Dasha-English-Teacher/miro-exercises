2. Read the next part of the story. What happened?

Reveal the story

Clara woke to soft light and an amazing view of palm trees and a beach. But there were metal bars on the window. Clara looked around and saw cold grey walls which revealed the truth—she was in a prison cell. And a terrible one, too.

An old woman was sitting opposite her on an old bed. Clara asked, “Do you have a pen and paper?”

“¿Qué necesitas?” the woman replied. Clara panicked—Spanish? She paused, trying to remember school lessons she used to skip. “Libro... paper... to write!”

The woman angrily threw her a pencil and a notepad. “No soy tu sirvienta,” she shouted and sat back on the bed.

“Gracias... so much gracias,” Clara whispered.
