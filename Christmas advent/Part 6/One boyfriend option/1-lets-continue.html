Reveal the police report

Date: 21.12.24
Location: 128 Fernwood Dr.
Report number: 1478-24
Incident: Homicide

At 8:00 AM, officers responded to 128 Fernwood Dr. after reports of strange activity. Clara Davis was found with her boyfriend, Ashton Pikes, while a second man, Daniel Porter, was later found dead in a nearby alley.

Davis claimed Pikes arrived late, and she heard noises. She denied any involvement with Porter. Pikes, however, claimed he discovered she had been dating Porter and himself at the same time. Forensics indicated Porter’s death was not accidental. Davis and Pikes were taken in for questioning.

Useful vocabulary

respond to reports — take action after receiving information
deny any involvement — say you had nothing to do with an incident
forensics — scientific examination of evidence
be taken in for questioning — be brought to a police station to answer questions
