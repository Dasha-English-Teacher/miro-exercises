Two Boyfriends, One Wish

Read the story and answer the questions.

1. Read what happened the next morning

Clara woke up the next morning, still in shock from everything that had happened. She walked to the kitchen, and her jaw dropped.

Sitting at the table were two men. The man from yesterday, the one who worked at the warehouse, was there as usual. But beside him was someone else—a tall, handsome younger man, looking as if he had always been there.

“Good morning, Clara,” he said.

Clara froze in the doorway. “What... why are you both here?” she asked.

The two men looked at each other and burst into laughter, as if she’d asked the most obvious question in the world. The first man chuckled and said, “Why wouldn’t we be here?”

The second man laughed too. “Yeah, it’s the weekend, so we’re both home.”

Clara looked from one man to the other and couldn’t understand how this was her reality now. Both men just kept eating breakfast, as if everything was perfectly normal.

“This is crazy,” she muttered to herself. Clara sat down trying to wrap her head around the situation. She had wished for a more handsome boyfriend, but now she had two. And they both seemed completely fine with it.

Did Clara’s wish come true?
