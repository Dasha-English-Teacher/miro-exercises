Christmas Advent · Lesson 5 · Clara prettier

A Different Reflection

Read what happened the next morning and retell the story.

1. Did Clara’s wish come true?

Clara woke up. She wasn't in her room—she was on an airplane, sitting in a fancy seat in first class. Beside her was a man, smiling and talking to her in a language Clara didn’t understand. She tried to follow the conversation, but the words flew past her. People all around her were speaking the same language, laughing and chatting.

Clara felt confused and out of place. The man beside her noticed her silence and gave her a puzzled look, but Clara could only smile awkwardly, unsure of what to say. She couldn't understand anything, and the language felt completely foreign.

Feeling trapped, Clara quickly stood up and excused herself, heading to the restroom. When she looked at herself in the mirror, her eyes widened. The face staring back at her was nothing like the Clara she knew. She was a tall woman, with smooth skin and striking features.

As she stood there, trying to make sense of it all, she realized something: the language the man had been speaking, the one she hadn't understood, was Spanish. The confusion deepened. Clara had wished to be prettier, but this? This was more than she had ever expected.

Did her wish come true?
