A Wish on Wheels

Read the story and decide what really happened.

1. Read what happened the next morning

Clara stepped outside, eager to see her new car. But when she reached the driveway, her excitement turned to confusion. There it was, her brand-new car sitting right on top of her old car, as if it had just appeared out of nowhere.

The shiny new sports car was red, sparkling and perfect, but the old car underneath... it was crushed, the roof bent in and the windows shattered. The new car also suffered a little after the hit. Was there a hit? Clara didn’t hear anything. It looked like the new car had just spawned there, landing on top of the old one in a way that didn’t make sense. She stood there, staring at the wreckage, completely out of words.

How did this happen? She had parked the old hatchback just fine the night before, and now this? It was as though the universe had decided to mess with her again. She reached out to touch the new car, trying to figure out if this was real or if she was just imagining everything. It was very real.

Did Clara’s wish come true?
